When to bring me in

A consequential decision needs a senior owner.

  • A product is approaching launch, but product, commercial and operations teams are not aligned.
  • A company is entering a new market and needs an executable entry model.
  • A complex B2B product needs a focused proposition, roadmap or pricing model.
  • Leadership or an investor needs an independent review before committing capital.
  • Senior product or programme ownership is temporarily missing.
